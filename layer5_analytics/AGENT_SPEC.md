# Layer 5: Analytics & Reporting — Agent Specification & Guidelines

## 🎯 Layer Scope & Responsibilities
Layer 5 is the **highest-level analytical and reporting layer**.
It sits above the operational layers to audit system performance, quantify financial savings, evaluate forecast accuracy, and produce human-readable digests.

### What Layer 5 DOES:
1. **Financial Savings Tracking:**
   - Calculates savings generated by dynamic load shifting vs. unoptimized baseload or standard flat contracts.
   - Audits battery arbitrage yields (strictly when $\Delta P \ge €0{,}115/\text{kWh}$).
2. **Efficiency & COP Monitoring:**
   - Evaluates seasonal heat pump COP trends for DHW (~2.04) and Space Heating (~4.80).
   - Computes solar self-consumption ratio (% of generated PV kept on-site).
3. **Forecast-vs-Actual Variance Auditing:**
   - Computes Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and RMSE comparing Day-Ahead EPEX/solar forecasts with verified InfluxDB hardware telemetry.
4. **Report Generation:**
   - Formats operational summaries for Telegram, Home Assistant dashboards, and Google Sheets.

### What Layer 5 MUST NEVER DO:
- **NO Hardware Actuation:** Layer 5 is purely read-only and analytical; it never triggers relays or sets setpoints.
- **NO Realtime Ingestion:** Layer 5 does not poll raw sensors or listen to MQTT sockets (that is Layer 1).
- **NO Mocking of Financial Data:** All cost and savings figures must be computed from real InfluxDB energy totals, genuine EPEX tariffs, and statutory tax markups.

## 🔌 Defined Interfaces
See `interfaces.py`:

- `IReportGenerator`: Daily and weekly digest formatting.
